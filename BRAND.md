# DeckSignal

**Find the signal in AI presentations.**

DeckSignal is the public project and website brand. Its Chinese descriptor is **AI 演示智鉴**, and its Chinese tagline is **看见工具之外的真实能力**.

## Naming architecture

- **DeckSignal Atlas** — the continuously updated tool and workflow landscape.
- **DeckSignal Evidence** — sources, generated artifacts, run manifests, and provenance.
- **OpenPPTBench** — the internal, reproducible benchmark and inspection engine.
- **DeckSignal Arena** — blinded human comparison and preference aggregation.
- **DeckSignal Index** — evidence-aware, scenario-specific rankings.

Use `DeckSignal` for the repository, website, publications, and community. Use `OpenPPTBench` only when referring specifically to the benchmark engine, its Python package, legacy schemas, or compatibility interfaces.
